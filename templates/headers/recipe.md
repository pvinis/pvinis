## Pasta Aglio e Olio

**Prep time:** 10 min | **Cook time:** 15 min | **Serves:** 1 hungry developer

1. Boil pasta (spaghetti, obvs)
2. Slice 6 cloves of garlic thin
3. Fry garlic in olive oil on low heat. Do NOT burn it.
4. Add red pepper flakes because you are brave
5. Toss pasta in the garlicky oil
6. Top with parsley, parmesan, and the existential dread of your abandoned repos
